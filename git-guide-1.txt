PART 3: Moving the changes to Staging(Locally)

Staging :-an intermediate stage where you can review, Adjust, remove changes                                                                           In Git, "stages" (or "the staging area") means you have marked specific
          changes to be included in your next commit.

      Think of it like packing a suitcase for a trip:

      Working directory = Your whole closet (all your clothes/changes)

      Staging area = The suitcase (you stage the items you want to bring)

      Commit = Zipping the suitcase and locking it (saving that exact selection    
               permanently)
